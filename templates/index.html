How it works

  1. Start the camera and allow access when asked.
  2. Calibrate: step out of frame for three seconds so Veil memorises the empty background.
  3. Pick your colour to match your cloth, and tune the tolerance.
  4. Step back in and hold up the cloth. Every pixel of that colour is swapped for the saved background.
